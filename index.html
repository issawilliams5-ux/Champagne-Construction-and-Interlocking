About

A career devoted to public trust and financial stewardship

John Bailey Church — known professionally as Bailey — is an Ottawa-based Canadian accounting and public-sector advisory leader. CPA Ontario lists his active designations as FCPA and FCA, and his professional profiles further recognize him as a Certified Internal Auditor (CIA).

He leads a Big Four firm's National Public Sector Accounting Advisory service line and serves as Leader of the firm's Public Sector ESG & Climate Reporting Centre of Expertise. His work centres on public-sector accounting, financial reporting, financial management, and auditing — serving the Government of Canada, provincial governments, crown corporations, municipalities, universities, not-for-profits, and for-profit entities engaged in public-sector transactions.

With more than two decades of experience in the public-sector environment, Bailey pairs deep technical authority with a commitment to transparency, stewardship, and the public interest.

ESG & Sustainability

Leading sustainability reporting for the public sector

A major focus of Bailey's recent work is sustainability reporting and ESG for governments and public institutions.

As Leader of the Public Sector ESG & Climate Reporting Centre of Expertise at a Big Four firm, he has worked with more than 300 governments on implementing asset retirement obligations, developing environmental roadmaps, and adapting International Sustainability Standards Board guidance for the public sector. He is a member of Canada's Auditing and Assurance Standards Board's Sustainability Assurance Committee and a board member of the Natural Assets Initiative.
