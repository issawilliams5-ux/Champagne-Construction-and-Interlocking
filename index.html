Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before booking your free consultation.

How long do projects take?

Timelines depend on scope. Smaller walkways or fire pits may take a few days, while full backyard transformations can run several weeks. You’ll receive a clear schedule during the planning stage.

Do you help with design?

Absolutely. Design collaboration is a core part of our process. We help you choose materials, layouts and features that fit your property, lifestyle and budget.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Ottawa and surrounding communities including Nepean, Alta Vista, Gloucester, Orleans, Kanata, Riverside South and Hunt Club. Reach out if your area isn’t listed.

What types of projects do you specialize in?

Interlock driveways, walkways, patios, fire pits, front entrances, stairs, retaining walls, custom stonework and complete backyard and outdoor living transformations.

How do I get a quote?

Simply request a free consultation using the form below or call us. We’ll arrange a visit, discuss your vision and provide a tailored proposal.
